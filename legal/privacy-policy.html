Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need to provide our products and services.

  • Contact and order details: your name, phone number, email address, delivery and billing address, and the details of your orders.
  • Health information you choose to share: your answers to the MARCEL OSMF Self-Check, symptoms, mouth opening measurements, habits such as tobacco or areca nut use, photographs or reports you send us, and notes from consultations with the MARCEL Team or a MARCEL OSMF Consultant. We treat this as sensitive information.
  • Professional information: if you are a doctor or healthcare professional, your name, qualifications, registration details, clinic address and contact details shared through our enquiry or onboarding forms.
  • Messages: what you write to us through website forms, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: your device and browser type, IP address, pages visited and similar data collected through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by our payment partners. We do not see or store your full card, UPI or bank account details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders, including payment confirmation, shipping and customer support.
  • To answer your questions and give guidance through the MARCEL Team and MARCEL OSMF Consultants.
  • To book and manage consultations you request.
  • To show your self-check result and suggest a sensible next step. The self-check is preliminary and does not diagnose OSMF.
  • To connect you with an OSMF Specialist Doctor when you ask us to, and only with your consent.
  • To list participating doctors in the OSMF Specialist directory, only with their consent and with the details they agree to.
  • To send order updates and, if you agree, information about MARCEL products and care. You can opt out of promotional messages at any time.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent fraud, understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

Consent

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as described below.

  • Service providers who help us run the website and our business, such as our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment partners, courier and logistics partners, messaging services (including WhatsApp), email and analytics providers. They may use your information only to provide their services to us.
  • OSMF Specialist Doctors, when you ask to be connected with one. Doctors in our network are independent professionals and handle your information under their own professional duties.
  • Authorities, when we are required to by law, court order or a valid government request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers, our team or the public.
  • A future owner of our business, if all or part of it is sold, merged or transferred, under terms that protect your information.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and where a cookie banner is shown you can choose your preferences there. Some parts of the website may not work properly without cookies.

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting records. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.
